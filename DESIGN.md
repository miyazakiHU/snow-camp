# 潮風工房 — campaign direction

The direct-control prototype is being developed into an original three-island workshop game. The working title has not received trademark clearance.

## Preserve

- Touch dragging, keyboard movement and walking up to an activity.
- Visible resources, processing, delivery, revenue collection and investment.
- Workers that physically carry resources instead of abstract income multipliers.
- No dashboard, tower defense, external services, accounts, advertising or payments.

## Change

- Replace the winter hunting/restaurant setting with a coastal repair workshop.
- New repair-robot character, salvage piles, workbench, shipment dock, boats, canal and lighthouse.
- Three short stages: repair a boat; rebuild a canal gate with an optional shortcut bridge; restore a lighthouse and deliver visiting-boat orders.
- Stage completion, a route screen, replay, persistent completion records and one selectable tool for the next stage.
- Local money, staff and equipment restart when departing. Unlocked tools and completed islands persist.

## Safety and rights

Use only original procedural geometry, synthesized effects and system fonts in the game. Do not incorporate extracted advertising/game assets, names, logos, screenshots or audio. Keep the original renderer where appropriate. This is a documented design separation, not a legal clearance or trademark opinion.

Do not commit personal data, credentials, private email addresses, conversation transcripts, unrelated project files or player saves. The game must not send gameplay/save data to external services.

## Delivery

Develop on this feature branch, test the browser-served game, and open a pull request against main. The published game is unchanged until merge. Preserve the original browser save key and store the new campaign in its own namespace.
